## Research Focus & Methodology

My research examines integral kernels, differential operators, and rigorous interval/ball enclosures in analytic number theory. Across the fifteen-paper series indexed below, every manuscript separates:

1. **Unconditional Analytical Identities & Ball-Arithmetic Certificates:** Exact Wigner phase-space representations, parity-reduced transverse Wronskians, harmonic nodal identities, Pólya–Schur multiplier phase transitions, and rigorous `FLINT/Arb` (`python-flint`) and `mpmath.iv` interval enclosures.
2. **Conditional Local Jets & Perturbation Formulas:** Asymptotic or local expansions whose hypotheses (such as absence of interior critical points or zero-free contour neighbourhoods) are stated explicitly.
3. **Counterexample Diagnostics & Structural Obstructions:** Quantitative tests on the self-dual Davenport–Heilbronn function $\Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}(z)$, Bohr–Voronin universality, Skewes phase alignment, and Selberg explicit-formula obstructions demonstrating why purely harmonic, functional-equation, or finite-truncation identities cannot by themselves exclude off-critical zeros.

All fifteen papers share the canonical normalization for Riemann's completed zeta function $\Xi(t - \mathrm{i}d) := \xi(\frac{1}{2} + d + \mathrm{i}t)$ and its transverse Wronskian:

$$
\Xi(t - \mathrm{i}d) = 8\int _ 0^\infty \Phi(u)\cos\bigl(2(t - \mathrm{i}d)u\bigr)\mathrm{d}u, \qquad W(d,t) = \frac{1}{2}\partial _ d\lvert\Xi(t - \mathrm{i}d)\rvert^2 = 16\int _ 0^\infty y\sinh(2dy)I(y,t)\mathrm{d}y = d\widetilde{W}(d,t).
$$

Each standalone repository includes the AMS-LaTeX source (`paper/*.tex`), compiled `pdfTeX` manuscript (`paper/*.pdf`), standalone high-precision verification and figure-generation script (`scripts/verify_*.py`), and citation metadata.

### Thematic Synopsis

* **Papers I–IV (Wigner Phase Space, Parity Reduction, Nodal Geometry & First-Order De Branges Kernels):** Expresses $I(y,t) = 2\pi\mathcal{W} _ \Phi(y/2, 2t)$ and isolates strip zeros of $\Phi(u)$ inside $\lvert\mathrm{Im}(u)\rvert < \pi/8$ (**Paper I**); eliminates the $d = 0$ coordinate singularity via $\widetilde{W}(d,t) = W(d,t)/d$ and Jensen–Laguerre moment identities $c _ {2m-1}(t) = m L _ m(\Xi;t)$ (**Paper II**); establishes the harmonic nodal dichotomy and transverse integral identity along $\lbrace\mathrm{Im} \Xi = 0\rbrace$ (**Paper III**); and analyzes $E _ \alpha = \Xi - \mathrm{i}\alpha\Xi'$, higher-multiplicity coalescence jets, and truncated prime-power multipliers (**Paper IV**).
* **Papers V–X (Shell Dilation, Sieved Kernels, Zero-Free Region Kernels & Bilinear $L^2$ Bounds):** Derives logarithmic theta-shell translations, strip-holomorphic partitions, and Stieltjes $(\ln t)^3$ diagonal asymptotics (**Paper V**); contrasts Gonek–Hughes–Keating and entire exponential prime factors on $(X, X^2]$ against functional-equation and Selberg explicit-formula obstructions (**Paper VI**); analyzes mirror-paired Poisson–Hadamard kernels and Trudgian Stieltjes bounds (**Paper VII**); proves an exact rank-2 factorization of symmetric Dirichlet Wronskian cross-terms alongside Preissmann $L^2$ bounds and pointwise Gallagher obstructions (**Paper VIII**); derives a shifted Mellin three-part bridge with pure-prime diagonal Stieltjes tail bounds on $X \ge \max(2, \sqrt{N(t)})$ (**Paper IX**); and pairs combinatorial Euler-sieve identities with Hadamard–Poisson flux detection (**Paper X**).
* **Papers XI–XV (De Bruijn–Newman PDE Hierarchy, Davenport–Heilbronn Counterexamples, $\beta^{\ast}$ & `FLINT/Arb` Bounds):** Proves the linear heat-flow hierarchy $(\partial _ \tau + \frac{1}{8}\partial _ t^2)L _ m = \frac{(m+1)(2m+1)}{4}L _ {m+1}$ and positive coalescence velocity under Rodgers–Tao scaling $\tau = t _ {\mathrm{DBN}}$ (**Paper XI**); shows that Hecke theta dilation and single-mode hyperbolic growth hold identically on $\Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}$ even at its off-line zero $(d _ {\ast}, \gamma _ {\ast}) \approx (0.308517, 85.699348)$ (**Paper XII**); proves cross-term-free second-order splitting $K _ {\alpha,\beta}(w,w) = \frac{\alpha}{\pi}\widetilde{W} _ {0,1} + \frac{\alpha\beta}{\pi}\widetilde{W} _ {1,2}$, exposes the Davenport–Heilbronn kernel blind spot above $\beta _ {\mathrm{blind}}^{(1)} \approx 0.053495$, and proves unconditionally via Rouché's theorem on Bohr-almost-periodic circles that $\beta^{\ast}(\Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}) = +\infty$ (**Paper XIII**); proves unconditional 50%–50% Green–Dirichlet–Neumann chamber flux equipartition, places local nodal exclusion within Craven–Csordas–Smith and Ki–Kim index theory, and evaluates closed-form $\mathrm{Li} _ 2(2\theta)$ prime-simplex integrals (**Paper XIV**); and classifies Pólya–Schur multipliers $G(-\partial _ z^2)\Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}$ across three Stieltjes regimes (including tower-lacunary obstructions and strict threshold separation $h _ {\mathrm{global}}^{\ast}(G _ {\mathrm{cosh}}; \Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}) < \frac{311}{500} < h _ {\mathrm{global}}^{\ast}(G _ {\mathrm{sinc}}; \Xi _ {\mathrm{DH}}){}$) while proving strict unconditional `FLINT/Arb` bounds $\Lambda _ \zeta \le \frac{1}{5} < \frac{1153}{5000} < \Lambda _ {\mathrm{DH}} < 2(\sigma^{\ast} - \frac{1}{2})^2 = 0.769699\dots$ (**Paper XV**).

## Research Focus & Scientific Methodology

My research investigates fundamental problems in analytic number theory and mathematical physics through the interplay of **rigorous classical analysis, phase-space representations, and high-precision computational certification**.

Primary areas of interest:
* **Analytic Number Theory & Special Functions**: Jacobi theta functions, Mellin transforms, and integral representations of symmetric $L$-functions.
* **Phase-Space Methods & Harmonic Analysis**: Wigner distributions, transverse Wronskian operators, and saddle-point coalescence in oscillatory kernels.
* **Interval Arithmetic & Computer-Assisted Verification**: High-precision arbitrary-precision arithmetic (`mpmath`), GPU-accelerated Lipschitz bounds, and topological interval certification.
* **Nonlinear & Discrete Dynamics**: Discrete dynamical systems, spectral invariants, and bounding techniques.

### Open Science & Reproducibility
All analytical derivations, numerical suites, and interactive simulations adhere to the **Egor Protocol** for open reproducibility: each project includes standalone test suites verifiable to high precision (30–60 decimal digits) and accessible visualization tools.

## Selected Work

### Transverse Wronskian & Phase-Space Analysis of Riemann's Kernel
An investigation into the transverse dynamics of Riemann's $\Phi(u)$ Jacobi-theta kernel across the critical line.
* **Key Techniques**: Wigner phase-space transforms, parity-reduced transverse Wronskians $\widetilde{W}(d,t) = W(d,t)/d$, and saddle-point coalescence along complex integration contours.
* **Certification**: GPU-accelerated Lipschitz bounding and multi-decimal certified interval evaluations ($>30$ decimal digits) demonstrating sign-definiteness on compact domains.
* **Resources**: Interactive web simulations and reproducible Python suites hosted at [gameghost.be](https://gameghost.be).

## 🔬 Research Focus & Scientific Philosophy

I explore profound mathematical problems through the intersection of **analytic theory, phase-space representations, and high-precision computational certification**. My work combines rigorous classical analysis with modern high-performance numerical verification to investigate structural symmetries, spectral behavior, and boundary phenomena.

Key areas of interest:
* **Analytic Number Theory & Special Functions**: Jacobi theta functions, Mellin transforms, and integral representations of symmetric L-functions.
* **Phase-Space Methods & Harmonic Analysis**: Wigner-Ville distributions, transverse Wronskians, and saddle-point coalescence in oscillatory kernels.
* **Interval Arithmetic & Rigorous Verification**: High-precision arbitrary-precision arithmetic (`mpmath`), GPU-accelerated Lipschitz bounds, and topological degree / interval certification.
* **Nonlinear Dynamics & Conjectural Bounds**: Discrete dynamical systems (Collatz-type systems), spectral invariants, and extremal value distribution.

> **Open-Science & Academic Rigor**:

## 📚 Selected Research & Projects

### 🌟 [The Transverse Wronskian & Phase-Space Analysis of Riemann's Kernel](https://gameghost.be)
An analytical and numerical investigation into the transverse dynamics of Riemann's $\Phi(u)$ Jacobi-theta kernel across the critical line.
* **Core Techniques**: Wigner phase-space transforms, parity-reduced transverse Wronskians $\widetilde{W}(d,t) = W(d,t)/d$, and saddle-point coalescence along complex integration contours.
* **Verification**: GPU-accelerated Lipschitz bounding and multi-decimal certified interval evaluations ($>30$ decimal digits) demonstrating sign-definiteness on compact domains.
* **Resources**: Interactive web simulations and reproducible Python suites hosted at [gameghost.be](https://gameghost.be).
